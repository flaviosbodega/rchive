RChive

RChive is research, identification, cataloguing, collection-management, and commerce-workflow software for antiques, collectibles, jewelry, fine art, and other valuable objects.

About RChive

RChive is a desktop-first research platform built to bring object research, documentation, identification, records, and selling workflows into one system.

What it does

  • Researches objects, makers, artists, marks, and signatures
  • Organizes research sources and supporting evidence
  • Builds structured records for objects and collections
  • Assists with identification and visual research
  • Stores images, descriptions, materials, techniques, marks, and provenance information
  • Supports market research and pricing workflows
  • Prepares information for marketplace and commerce workflows
  • Combines local desktop capabilities with selected hosted services
